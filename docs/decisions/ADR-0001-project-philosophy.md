# ADR-0001: Project Philosophy

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Court Ready is for one recreational pickleball player who wants more energy, strength, mobility, recovery, and sustainable habits. Generic fitness tracking would add noise and decision fatigue.

## Decision

Build a calm, single-user, mobile-first dashboard that answers "What should I do today?" Do not add generic tracking, social features, analytics, accounts, or backend systems during the foundation sprint.

## Consequences

- Product scope remains intentionally narrow.
- Daily guidance can be highly opinionated.
- Future features must prove they reduce decision fatigue.
