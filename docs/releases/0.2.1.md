# Release 0.2.1

Personalisation pass. The 0.2 plan and targets were placeholders; this replaces them with the athlete's actual schedule, stats, and supplement stack.

## Program

The week is now built around playing on Saturday and Sunday.

| Slot | Day | Template |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Monday | Full Recovery |
| 1 | Tuesday | Strength Reset (lower) |
| 2 | Wednesday | Mobility & Reset |
| 3 | Thursday | Push, Pull, Carry (upper) |
| 4 | Friday | Easy Movement |
| 5 | Saturday | Court Day |
| 6 | Sunday | Court Day Two |

- Monday recovers from the weekend rather than opening it with strength.
- Friday tapers instead of taxing the legs the day before play.
- Sunday is a distinct template: a longer warm-up and an explicit post-play cool-down, because day two of back-to-back play is where things tweak.

## Adaptation rule change

The 0.2 rule "played yesterday and today is a play day, so downshift" would have fired **every Sunday**, cancelling the intended back-to-back weekend.

Playing yesterday now costs one point of readiness on a demanding day instead of overriding it:

- Readiness 9 or 8 on Sunday after Saturday play: you play.
- Readiness 7 on Sunday after Saturday play: drops to 6 and downshifts to mobility.
- The same readiness of 7 without having played: you play.

This keeps the signal meaningful while respecting a schedule that stacks play on purpose.

## Targets

For 6'0", 220 lb, 52-year-old male:

- Protein target raised from 130g to **160g**, roughly 1.6g/kg. Set at the upper end deliberately, because protein requirements rise with age.
- Water target raised from 8 to **12** glasses, 14 on court days. Creatine raises fluid needs slightly.
- Protein now increments in 20g steps rather than 10g, putting the target at eight taps and matching the water card.

Targets are documented in `lib/dashboard-data.ts` with their derivation, so future changes are informed rather than arbitrary.

## Supplements

Replaced the inherited 0.1 stack (Vitamin D, Omega-3, Magnesium) with the real one:

- Multivitamin, with breakfast.
- Creatine, 5g daily, any time, training or not.
- Protein shake, as the simplest route to the daily target.

## Testing

- 81 unit tests, up from 76. New coverage for the weekend schedule, the second court day's cool-down obligation, and the readiness-penalty behaviour in both directions.
- Re-verified in a browser.

## Note

These are baselines, not prescriptions, and the app states as much. Worth a conversation with a doctor given a new supplement stack.
